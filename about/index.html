Hi, I’m Yoonjung,
a UX designer working across digital and physical experiences.

I use research to understand what people do, why they do it, and where their experiences fall short. I bring a sensitivity to form, space, and movement to UX, turning insights into interactions people can see, touch, and try. My work spans physical products, digital interfaces, and immersive prototypes.

I’m currently pursuing a master’s in Human Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington.

My approach

How I approach design

Field research · User interviews

01

Understand the why.

I'm drawn to the reasons behind everyday behaviors—what people adapt to, work around, or leave unsaid. Research helps me uncover those details and decide what to design for.

XR prototyping · Physical models

02

Think through making.

I think in three dimensions. From physical models to interactive prototypes, making helps me explore how an experience should look, feel, and respond—and gives people something concrete to react to.

Co-design · Team synthesis

03

Make room for every voice.

I bring energy to team discussions and help people feel comfortable sharing ideas and concerns. I enjoy connecting different perspectives so we can find a direction together.
