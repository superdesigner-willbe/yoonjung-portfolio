UX/UI · INTERACTION DESIGN · SHIPPED PRODUCT

Bringing textbook lessons into action.

Designing interactive activities, textbook tools, and learning insights — all implemented in the digital textbook.

My Role

Interaction & UI design
Insight card planning contribution

Scope

Learning activities · Viewer tools
Progress UI · Data visualization & copy

The Context

The textbook content was already defined. My role was to shape how students interacted with it and how learning information appeared on screen.

The Challenge

The experience needed to let students apply concepts through interaction, while making learning progress and status understandable.

Only publicly released work is shown. Research findings and internal UX documentation are omitted due to confidentiality.

Design Question

How could fixed textbook content become interactive practice with understandable learning status?

01 Interactive Learning

I turned textbook content into tasks students could act on

I designed click responses, scene transitions, and the timing of images and questions around the learning activity.

Explore the scene through interaction

02 Textbook Viewer

I designed tools to revisit pages and record thoughts while learning

My viewer work included bookmarking, memo features, and progress indicators.

Bookmarks

Keep useful pages within reach

Progress at a glance

Show completion in numbers and form

A percentage and bar present completion numerically and visually

03 Learning Insights

I paired learning data with readable explanations

I contributed to insight card planning, chart presentation, and wording to communicate learning status.

Outcome

From design to implementation

My learning interactions, viewer features, progress UI, and the insight cards I contributed to were all implemented in the digital textbook.

Post-launch learning and business results were not available to me after I left the company.
